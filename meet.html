Meet face to face

Create a private room or join with a short code. Media stays peer-to-peer; only signaling goes through your server.

How it works
1. Deploy the signaling server (server/ folder) on Railway, Render, Fly.io or any VPS.
2. Paste its public HTTPS URL above and click Save.
3. Create a room → share the short code with participants.
Camera & microphone are requested when you enter the room. Free, no watermark, no time limit.
