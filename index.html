GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Is the screen recorder free?

The app is designed for free recording without a watermark or an imposed recording time limit. Browser and device capabilities may affect available features.

Can I record my microphone and screen?

Yes. Choose the available screen, microphone, and camera sources before recording. Your browser will ask for the required permissions.

Can I create meetings with live transcripts?

Yes. Open a meeting room and enable live transcription where supported. Make sure everyone knows and agrees before sharing their speech.

Where are my recordings saved?

Recordings are handled in your browser. Download or save each recording to your device so you keep a copy.
